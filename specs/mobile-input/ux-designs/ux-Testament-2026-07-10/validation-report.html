Testament Mobile Input — UX Design Validation Report

specs/mobile-input/ux-designs/ux-Testament-2026-07-10/

The spine pair is mechanically excellent and substantively holed. Every token resolves, every source is on disk, section orders are canonical, and the trust-boundary claim (“zero server change”) is literally true — no task touches src/server or src/shared, and MovePayload.walk already exists on the wire. The client-side legality recompute is a legitimately precedented render hint, not I1 game logic.

What fails is not the architecture but three things the spec asserted without checking. It invented a testing convention and cited it as established precedent, leaving its first task with no real verification. It specified a Probe button that cannot form a legal wire message, because ProbePayload requires a stimulus the single-slot design has no way to choose. And its accessibility floor does not hold: the two colour pairs carrying the HUD's most important state changes — the walk/run register (1.52:1) and the refusal flash (1.26:1) — fail the WCAG 3:1 threshold badly enough to be invisible, while “40 logical px ≈ 48dp” is true only at exactly integer factor 3 on a ~400dpi phone.

One deeper risk is latent rather than present. The reserved DODGE slot inherits the cluster's founding doctrine — a control exists only while its action is legal — and combat.md gates dodging on reading the Omen. Applied literally by a future implementer, the button's appearance becomes the Omen tell, and the HUD performs the read the player was supposed to earn. Pillar 3, defeated by an affordance. It is cheap to fence off now, while the grammar is being written.

Reviewer — Accessibility

broken
The floor is not level. Two of the HUD's most important state signals — the walk/run register (1.52:1) and the server-refusal flash (1.26:1) — fail the WCAG 3:1 non-text threshold so badly they are effectively invisible, and the register's only fallback is a haptic tick with no in-app toggle. The “40 logical px ≈ 48dp” claim holds only at exactly factor 3 / 400dpi. The spine also promises a screen-reader-nameable surface Godot 4.7 cannot deliver on mobile.

high

The tap-target floor does not hold across devices

DESIGN.md spacing.tap_min; R135
40 logical px = 48.0dp only at factor 3 + 400dpi; 41.9dp on an iPhone 13 Pro Max (under Apple's 44pt); 32dp at factor 2 / 400dpi. action_size 44 is only 35dp at factor 2. The integer factor tracks pixel height, not density.
Fix: Derive at runtime from DisplayServer.screen_get_dpi(), clamping to ceil(48 * dpi/160 / factor). Keep 40 as a lower bound, not the target.
high

The refusal flash is nearly isoluminant

DESIGN.md colors.action_denied; Flow 2
1.26:1 against a 3:1 requirement. Priority is inverted — the low-stakes press ack is ~12:1 while the high-stakes refusal is a whisper. For a protanope the red collapses toward the stone.
Fix: Make refusal a luminance event, not a hue event: flash to a bright value, or invert to a light face with a red glyph.
high

The walk/run register is colour-only and fails 3:1

DESIGN.md “Gold intensity = the speed register”
1.52:1. Directly contradicts EXPERIENCE.md's own “colour is never the only channel.” The register feeds field pressure (D3) — not cosmetic.
Fix: Give the registers a shape difference: solid inner ring for walk, a second concentric or ticked rim for run.
high

No motor accommodations exist

design.md geometry block
No one-handed mode, no stick-size/deadzone/position customisation, no remapping, no switch path. A floating stick requires a sustained drag — hostile to tremor, limited grip endurance, or one usable hand.
Fix: Expose deadzone and radius as settings; offer a fixed-stick option; document a switch fallback even if deferred.
high

Haptics are load-bearing with no in-app control

EXPERIENCE.md Game Feel
The boundary tick is the way “the register is felt, not read”, yet haptics are deferred to a device pass with no toggle. With system haptics off, the register has no cue at all — its visual channel already fails.
Fix: Add an in-app haptics toggle; never let haptics solely carry a gameplay-relevant state.
high

The screen-reader claim is not implementable

EXPERIENCE.md Accessibility Floor
Godot 4.7 has no TalkBack/VoiceOver bridge (AccessKit work is desktop-scoped), and the HUD controls are custom _draw Controls exposing nothing to any a11y tree.
Fix: Soften to what is true — caption text exists per role/state, positioning a future glyph-free mode — and do not count it as satisfied.
medium

Tap-to-move was rejected to protect an unbuilt combat window

D2; EXPERIENCE.md anti-patterns
ATTACK/DODGE have no wire intent and the slots draw nothing. An accessibility affordance is denied today on the strength of an unbuilt system.
Fix: Reconsider tap-to-move as an a11y option now, auto-disabling once a timed dodge exists on the wire.
medium

Nothing teaches the player the invisible left zone exists

Flow 1; DESIGN.md
First-run is unspecified. “The empty screen is the design” is fine for a returning player and a real barrier for a new one.
Fix: A one-time, dismissible ghost-stick or zone-shimmer cue.
medium

Reduced motion does not cover HUD control transitions

EXPERIENCE.md; Flow 1 step 4
Flow 1 has the Interact button “fade in” while DESIGN.md forbids the stick fading. The button fade and cluster pop-in are uncovered by any setting.
Fix: Fold HUD control transitions under the reduced-motion lever; reconcile the contradiction.
medium

No path to larger text for low-vision players

DESIGN.md typography
OS zoom is ignored by a fullscreen GL viewport; raising the integer factor zooms the world and shrinks the visible field.
Fix: Sanction an optional 2×/3× integer caption scale — crisp, on-grid, and not “sub-font scaling”.
low

1px outline is 2 physical px at factor 2

DESIGN.md colors.outline
High-contrast, but physically thin as the sole edge cue for low-vision users.
Fix: Scale the outline with the integer factor, or offer a 2-logical-px option.

Reviewer — Input scheme & engine feasibility

thin
The two pure functions are arithmetically sound — the hysteresis band is not inverted and the & 7 two's-complement wrap is correct, contrary to the usual suspicion. The danger is elsewhere: an unmodelled Probe→stimulus gap, a Y-axis sign trap in a comment, directional dither at octant boundaries, and multitouch assumptions true only under an unstated discipline.
critical

The Probe cluster button cannot produce a valid PROBE payload

design.md “Legality → cluster”
ProbePayload = { stimulus: Stimulus } (fieldMessages.ts:19); the server gates per-stimulus (probe.ts:32). The existing UI draws one button per stimulus (main.gd:474-478). Failing case: a Seeker carrying Censer(FLAME)+Salt(SALT) taps the one Probe button — the client cannot choose, so it sends a malformed PROBE {} or silently picks one.
Fix: Either N Probe slots (one per carried stimulus) or a stimulus chooser on tap. Unresolved on both spines. Must be designed before T151.
high

The octant comment will invert vertical movement

design.md quantize8: “# 0..7, E counter-clockwise”
Godot screen space is Y-down, so increasing angle() sweeps visually clockwise. Failing case: thumb pushed straight down → angle()=+PI/2 → octant 2; a Y-up-authored table returns Vector2i(0,-1) and the Seeker walks up.
Fix: Delete “counter-clockwise”; author OCTANT = [E, DR, D, DL, W, UL, U, UR] with D=(0,1); assert quantize8(Vector2(0,10)) == Vector2i(0,1).
high

“A held stick sends nothing” is false — direction spam is unbounded

R130 AC; design.md P73; main.gd:641
Hysteresis covers walk/run only, never direction. Failing case: a thumb resting at ≈22.5° micro-wobbles ±0.5° per frame; v alternates (1,0)↔(1,1) and a MOVE is sent every frame. The keyboard has no analogue.
Fix: Add directional hysteresis — require the angle to cross an octant boundary by ±N° before re-quantizing.
high

set_input_as_handled() does not suppress the emulated mouse event

EXPERIENCE.md Interaction Primitives; R134
A finger-0 touch generates an independent InputEventMouseButton. Failing case: Extract implemented as a themed Button → one tap sends EXTRACT twice.
Fix: State as an invariant: field-HUD controls are IGNORE + manually hit-tested, never Godot Buttons wired to pressed. Only popup controls may be.
high

touch_hud._input must gate on _menu_open

main.gd:163; design.md “Touch routing”
mouse_filter governs mouse/GUI propagation only — it has no effect on InputEventScreenTouch delivery. Failing case: open Quartermaster, tap the panel's left half → a phantom stick ring spawns behind the dimmer.
Fix: Early-return from touch_hud._input whenever _menu_open, mirroring the existing _send_move_intent guard.
medium

_send_move_intent is keyboard-hardcoded

main.gd:626-645
It computes v from Input.is_physical_key_pressed via _dir_axis; there is no seam to inject a touch vector. “One send path, two producers” is not a drop-in, and P71 is at risk if done naively.
Fix: Refactor to take (dir, walk, active) from whichever producer the input scheme owns; keep the single send_message(MOVE, …) call site.
medium

emulate_touch_from_mouse is not set

project.godot
Default is false, so a desktop mouse produces no InputEventScreenTouch — playtest items M1/M2/M3/M5/M6 are un-runnable as written.
Fix: Set input_devices/pointing/emulate_touch_from_mouse=true (or a debug-only toggle) as part of T154/T156.
low

Self-check boundary rows must respect round()'s tie rule

tasks.md T148
round(0.5)=1, round(-0.5)=-1; an exact octant midpoint resolves to one neighbour. A “both boundaries” assertion will spuriously fail.
Fix: Test boundary±ε for the two neighbours; test the midpoint once against round()'s actual result.
low

The ring reports intent, not server speed

EXPERIENCE.md “The register is felt”
Cannot desync today (no server walk-rejection path), but the ring would lie if a server clamp were ever added.
Fix: Note only.

Reviewer — Canon & trust boundary

adequate
Trust-boundary discipline is genuinely strong: “zero server change” is literally true, and the client-side legality recompute is a legitimately-precedented render hint, not I1 game logic. Two things keep it from clean — a cited testing precedent that exists nowhere, and a reserved DODGE slot that points straight at a Pillar-3 trait-tell.
critical

The cited boot self-check precedent does not exist

requirements.md:119; tasks.md T148
The keepout seed=<s> ok=<bool> string appears in exactly one place: specs/notice-board/tasks.md:165 — an unchecked, unbuilt task. No self-check harness exists in any client code. The spec invented a verification mechanism and back-dated it as convention, so under spec-workflow T148 has no real test.
Fix: Either build the self-check as a first, real deliverable and stop calling it precedent, or verify the pure functions through the established MCP-playtest + get_debug_output path.
high

The reserved DODGE slot points at a Pillar-3 violation

D1/R131 vs combat.md:29-30,40; R137/P76
The cluster's doctrine is appearance == legality. A dodge is only useful during the Omen window, so a future implementer draws DODGE then — and the button's presence becomes the Omen tell, replacing the read the player was supposed to earn. A Sign readout by another name.
Fix: Record now: the ATTACK/DODGE slots, when populated, are gated on the Seeker's capability (kit/perception), never on the live state of any Incarnate sign. DODGE is drawn iff the Seeker carries the perception to read the Omen; its presence is identical whether or not an Omen is firing.

Fix: Palette-locked pixel assets (Aseprite or a gen_*.py sheet) blitted with Nearest, or a deliberately pixel-stepped draw.

Mechanical notes

  • All source paths resolve on disk (7/7, including the inherits: target). MovePayload confirmed at src/shared/src/fieldMessages.ts:26.
  • All token references resolve: 12/12 {token} refs, 17/17 backtick token names, 8/8 inherited colour names with matching hex. Zero missing-hex, zero broken cross-refs.
  • Component-list divergence is the one structural inconsistency: DESIGN has {floating_stick, action_button, action_cluster, caption}; EXPERIENCE has {floating stick, action button, action cluster, full-screen reader}. Toast appears in neither.
  • Frontmatter: DESIGN.md omits the spec-required name/description; inherits: is a non-spec key. Both match notice-board house style.
  • Verified-compliant, do not re-litigate: next_walk hysteresis is correct; quantize8's negative & 7 wrap is correct; display/window/handheld/orientation is the right key; both pure functions are static and table-verifiable; Extract legality is knowable client-side; walk cannot desync; hover-raise removal targets real code.

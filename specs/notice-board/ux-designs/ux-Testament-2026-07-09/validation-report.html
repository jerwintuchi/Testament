A strong, tightly-distilled spine pair. The palette is fully hex-locked, every prose {token} resolves, all named sources and both reused components (WaxSeal, ThreatPips) exist on disk, and both files hold canonical shape. A downstream implementer can source-extract the visual identity and the behavioral delta cleanly — the gaps are at the edges, not the spine.

But the legibility lens materially sharpens the central risk: the candlelit-gloom mood the reskin is built around is asserted, not guaranteed. The notice headline — the loudest, most load-bearing string — is painted in wax red #8F2F2A, the lowest-luminance ink in the palette; it fails WCAG on all but torch-lit paper and collapses to ~1.5:1 under gloom. Threat pips fail the 3:1 graphical floor, quietly undermining the "threat = pip count" non-color cue. No numeric contrast floor and no mandated live-notice paper tone make any of it testable. Two criticals and five highs — all fixable in spec, before Batch 1.

Behavior-drift passed clean (the reskin is correctly look-not-behavior; terminology is byte-identical to the tested specs/notice-board). Art-canon is sound but flags two conventions to honor: the torch glow / cobweb are specced as colored screen-blend against CLAUDE.md's grayscale ADD-blend VFX rule, and the unresolved Pillow-absent pipeline decision — which threatens the load-bearing glow generator — is not surfaced in the finalized spines.

critical

Headline is wax-red — the worst-contrast ink — with no tone floor

DESIGN.md Typography §2 / Colors
The sacred verb (the single most load-bearing string) is painted in the lowest-luminance ink: passes only on torch-lit highlight paper, fails on base (4.0), invisible under gloom (1.5).
Fix: render the headline in ink body #2A2115 (demote wax to non-text accent: seal, rule, drop-cap). If wax is kept, hard-AC it onto ≥highlight paper + 1px #12100C outline, measured ≥4.5:1.
critical

No numeric floor + no mandated live-notice paper tone

DESIGN.md Do's; EXPERIENCE.md Accessibility
Live-vs-flavor is a brightness axis, which permits a live notice at shadow tone — where body ink is 2.9:1 and a wax headline 1.5:1.
Fix: "A live notice renders at ≥ parchment.base, never shadow, with a torch/glow contribution; body & headline ≥ 4.5:1 vs composited local backing." Add a framebuffer-luminance AC to playtest.md on a worst-case seed.
high

Torch coverage of the four live anchor slots not guaranteed

DESIGN.md Layout
Two edge light sources, but live notices scatter across the whole board (the key-screen's RITE OF BANISHMENT sits bottom-center, between the pools). Legibility depends on torch reach, but nothing binds live anchors to the lit region.
Fix: constrain live anchor slots to a guaranteed-lit envelope, or give every live notice its own local "lit paper" backlight independent of the wall torches.
high

Threat pips fail 3:1 — the non-color threat cue is invisible

DESIGN.md Colors/Shapes
Gold-on-parchment is 1.6–3.2:1; the empty pip is outlined in an even lower tone. If pips can't be resolved, "state never by color alone" is silently defeated.
Fix: every pip (filled & empty) carries a 1px #12100C outline; empty pip = outlined-hollow diamond, not a faint interior.
medium

"Legibility beats mood" not enforceable against occlusion

DESIGN.md Don'ts; EXPERIENCE.md
At rest a live headline can sit under another notice's corner (raise is hover-only); cobweb/votive can land over a live notice. Enforced only by eyeball.
Fix: reserve each live notice's headline+target+seal+pip band as a deterministic keep-out rect no other notice/decay may overlap; bind decay props to corners proven empty of live anchors.
medium

Reduced-motion fallback may freeze the glow at its dim trough

EXPERIENCE.md Game Feel
The glow pool is the load-bearing light; naive animation:none pins it to the CSS base (dim end), leaving the reduced-motion board darker than the animated one. Godot has no auto prefers-reduced-motion — needs an explicit toggle.
Fix: with motion off, render the pool at fixed peak-equivalent brightness; only the pulse is decorative. Re-sample luminance AC with motion off.
medium

Flavor readability ambiguous — lore or wallpaper?

DESIGN.md Colors
Flavor text computes ~2.9:1 before a further 0.72 brightness cut (unreadable) — fine if pure atmosphere, but the authored strings read as intended lore.
Fix: pick one — (a) flavor is deliberately sub-threshold atmosphere (keep its strings non-load-bearing), or (b) hold it to ≥4.5:1. Don't leave it in the middle.
medium

No focus / keyboard floor; live hit-target can fall below minimum

EXPERIENCE.md Foundation
Popup opens with E but no focusable notices / focus-visible ring / seal focus state; a "small note" scatter variant can drop below the ~44px target, tilted and overlapped.
Fix: live notices focusable (Tab=reading order) with a ≥3:1 gold focus ring, Enter/Space activates; enforce a minimum un-occluded live footprint (≥44×44) in the layout function.
low

Origin colorblind cue + faint-seal visibility unstated

EXPERIENCE.md State Patterns
Origin is missing from "state never by color alone" (sigil shape must be the load-bearing cue, color redundant); a faint unsealed seal risks reading as no seal in gloom.
Fix: add Origin = sigil-shape to the list; keep the faint seal's ring/outline at full strength (≥3:1 edge) while only the fill is faint.

Art-canon compliance

review-art-canon.md · high 2 / medium 2 / low 2 (trait-containment clean)

Sound, ship after two fixes. No-Incarnate-art, trust boundary, size-not-tier, solemn-not-whimsy, and P65 flavor-inert are all clean and stated as hard don'ts. Two conventions need honoring before assets are authored.

high

Colored screen-blend glow/cobweb vs "grayscale ADD-blend VFX"

DESIGN.md torch/cobweb; EXPERIENCE.md Game Feel
A flame-colored screen-blend pool is a different technique from CLAUDE.md's canonical grayscale ADD-blend, and injects off-ramp intermediate colors.
Fix: spec the glow (and web/overlay) as a grayscale additive source tinted at runtime via modulate to the flame ramp (or a Light2D whose color is the flame ramp over a grayscale cookie), Nearest, integer-scaled.
high

Pillow-absent pipeline is OPEN and unsurfaced; threatens the load-bearing glow

.decision-log.md (last entry); DESIGN.md Do's
The assets needing true alpha/gradients — torch glow ("load-bearing"), cobweb, torn/deckled edges, foxing — are exactly what the Pass-1 stdlib zlib+struct path makes painful. A finalized-DESIGN reader wouldn't know the generator is undecided.
Fix: surface it in a DESIGN.md "Pipeline" note and resolve a-vs-b before Batch 1. If staying stdlib, confirm RGBA/gradient feasibility; else get Pillow approved (a toolchain env change) with the user first.
medium

Pixel integrity under seeded rotation / non-integer scale unaddressed

DESIGN.md Layout/Shapes
Arbitrary tilt + non-integer hover/reader scale of pixel sprites breaks the Nearest/integer-scale crispness mandate (edge shimmer, AA seams).
Fix: pre-bake a few rotated deckle variants (seeded pick) or a pixel-rotation shader; restrict enlarge/hover to integer scale steps; forbid angles that soften the headline below the floor.
medium

Palette-lock is directional, not absolute

DESIGN.md Colors/Do's
"Derive from these ramps" stops short of a no-off-ramp rule; blend/Light2D/modulate math produces colors between the 8 ramps.
Fix: "No pixel outside the Ash & Ember ramps; VFX tint uses the flame ramp only; any blended/lit output is quantized back to the locked palette before export."
low

"16×16-class" latitude

DESIGN.md Brand & Style
Consistent with CLAUDE.md's own wording, so acceptable.
Fix (optional): note frame/placard/tacks are authored on the 16px grid so "-class" isn't an excuse for half-pixel detail.
